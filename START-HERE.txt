APPROVED DIRECTION
Clean, sleek, bold, spacious, and personal. An outdoor community brand with the confidence of a clothing label. The owner loves the current draft; evolve it instead of redesigning it.
Warm off-white, charcoal, apron brown. Large condensed headlines. Actual hike photos. Full-screen hamburger navigation. No generic rounded-card grids, gradients, floating badges, decorative statistics, or canned adventure copy.

REFERENCE PREFERENCES
norda (https://nordarun.com/): spacious mountain imagery and immersive opening.
Bandit (https://banditrunning.com/): the owner likes its hamburger-menu approach.
Deus Ex Machina (https://deuscustoms.com/): very cool personality and setup, but simplify the crowding and avoid making this primarily a clothing website.
Gozney (https://www.gozney.com/): cool, but its treatment felt too similar to an AI-built website. Avoid that direction.
