02 / WHY IT MATTERSMORE PEOPLE. MORE PANCAKES.

THE TRAIL
BREAKFASTS
STAY FREE.

The mountains in Utah are grossly underappreciated next to the phones in our laps. We've bought into the idea that we can get people to look up and actually connect in nature — with social media, and with free pancakes on the trail.

The thing is, pancakes, griddles, gas, and videography aren't free. Catering is how we keep those trail breakfasts free. We price ourselves fairly cheap because we aren't in it for the money. We're in it for the community we're creating. Every catering job buys more mix, carries more gear, and gets more people into the mountains.
