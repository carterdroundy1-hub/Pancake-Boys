BEFORE YOU WRITEQUOTES ON REQUEST

A FEW
PLAIN ANSWERS.

Do you come to us?
Yes. We show up and cook breakfast on site — pancakes coming off the griddle while your people gather. We bring the griddles and cook in front of your group, not drop-off trays.
Who is this for?
Corporate mornings, fire crews and first responders, wedding mornings, and community or church events around Utah.
What do the prices mean?
The Trail, The Ridge, and The Summit start at $4, $5, and $6 a person. They are rough estimates, not a set price. Book the gathering and we will quote yours.
Can you do gluten-free?
Mention it in your notes and we will plan for it. Gluten-free is available on any tier when you ask.
When will we hear back?
We usually reply within a couple of days. Questions first? Email pancak3boys@gmail.com or send a note on Instagram.
